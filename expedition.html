Why

The unfinished why

The seed was planted on my 30th birthday. My Danish teacher gave me a book documenting a Dutchman’s journey from Amsterdam to Singapore. I remember looking at it and wondering: could I do something like that?

Years later, I’m finally finding out. I have reasons for leaving, but I don’t want to manufacture a perfectly polished answer before the journey begins. I want to notice how the why changes as I make my way south.

What

A moving inquiry

Across Europe

I’m travelling from Copenhagen to Barcelona under my own leg power, moving slowly enough to experience the places between the pins on a map.

Through people

I’ll speak with people I already know who have lived elsewhere—and people I meet along the road—about what belonging means to them.

In public

Films, field notes and unfinished ideas will emerge while I’m still moving. The expedition is the documentary’s first draft, shared in real time.

The question

Where—and with whom—do we belong?

Can home be a country, a language, a person, a memory—or something we carry and continually remake?

I’m not looking for one definitive answer. I’m collecting parallels: between people who left, people who stayed, the places that shaped them and the places they chose.
